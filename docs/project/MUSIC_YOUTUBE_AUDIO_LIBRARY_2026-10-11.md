# Licensed music integration: source-focused handoff

The shipped config/music_library.json is empty. Music must have a valid licence, commercial-use permission and a verified SHA-256. Owner-licensed files stay outside Git, under config/music/ or STUDIO_MUSIC_LIBRARY. Inspect backend/app/media/music_library.py and the mix/quality path. A YouTube Audio Library account is an Owner-managed external integration; credentials, channel IDs, private research and downloads are omitted.

This summary replaces private operational records only in the GitHub copy.
