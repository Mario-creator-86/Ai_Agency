# LLM call sites: the complete catalog

> **Scope.** Every place in `backend/app` that writes a question to a language model and uses the
> answer (an "LLM call site"), grouped by pipeline phase: who speaks, what is asked, what shape
> the answer must have, how far the answer is trusted, what code does with it, what happens when
> the model fails, and whether it can lead to money being spent.
>
> **Method.** Working tree of 2026-10-12 (branch `codex/simple-pixel-studio`, HEAD `3d49e9a`;
> the backend files are unchanged since `b00083c`). Sources: the static inventory
> (`docs/project/backend_map/inventory.json`, `STATS.md`, generated by `scripts/backend_map.py`),
> the eighteen verified subsystem maps under `docs/project/backend_map/subsystems/`, and direct
> reading of the code for every claim that was disputed or uncertain. Nothing was run except
> read-only `grep` and an AST count; no model, provider, test or studio process was started.
>
> **Vocabulary.** *Trust* of a call site's answer: **FINAL** (acted on as is after schema
> validation), **CHECKED_BY_CODE** (code verifies content and can refuse, clamp or override),
> **ADVISORY** (recorded or weighed; code or the Owner has the final say), **INFORMATIONAL**
> (human-readable text with no control effect). The human is "the Owner"; employees keep the
> code's pronouns (the Director is "she").
>
> Companion files: `LLM_VS_RULES.md` (how much of the system is LLM), `LLM_ROUTING.md` (models,
> providers, the gateway step by step), `DECISION_AUTHORITY.md` (who decides what),
> `RUNTIME_FLOW.md` (what runs when).

## 1. Summary

- **One door.** Every language-model call leaves through
  `app.agents.services.run_agent_task` (`backend/app/agents/services.py:733`). It composes the
  employee's identity as the system prompt, runs the Policy Kernel (daily token ceiling, USD
  budget, STOP AUTONOMY, autonomy mode, lifecycle, model availability, one live run, required
  capabilities, provider health, adapter present), calls the adapter and records the run.
  Four thin gateways sit on top of it: `structured_run` (`backend/app/agents/structured.py:76`,
  schema-validated, at most two attempts), `run_with_strategy`
  (`backend/app/agents/ensemble.py:182`, multi-model strategies), `reviews._run`
  (`backend/app/agents/reviews.py:121`) and `prompt_agent._run`
  (`backend/app/media/prompt_agent.py:2948`).
- **37 call sites, and no others.** Cross-check by `grep` on the working tree: every
  `run_agent_task(` call in `backend/app` sits inside one of the 16 direct call sites below or
  inside a gateway (`ensemble.py:222`, `reviews.py:132`, `structured.py:121`,
  `prompt_agent.py:2963`); every gateway call sits inside one of the other 21 call sites; and
  the only call of a provider's `generate` is the gateway's own (`services.py:929`). The groups
  found no additional call site; they found additional *paths* to existing ones that the static
  graph does not resolve (section 5). This count is the snapshot's (backend as of `b00083c`).
  Commit `20abffd`, made after the snapshot, adds a 38th call site,
  `app.media.second_look._look` (the Director's second look at a picture QC refused, before a
  re-roll or retry is bought); `README.md` §8 describes it.
- **Trust tally.** CHECKED_BY_CODE **24**, FINAL **5**, ADVISORY **5**, INFORMATIONAL **3**
  (section 2 per site; section 6 explains where this differs from earlier tallies).
- **Spend.** No model answer buys anything by itself. 6 call sites' answers can trigger or
  reopen a paid media/GPU purchase that then passes the money gates (`may_act`, the Owner's
  paid-media rule, budgets, caps, the film ceiling, the purchase lease); 15 shape what later paid
  steps buy (topic, claims, script, prompts, default model); 16 cost tokens only. Every call
  costs tokens, priced from the Owner's `ProviderPrice` rows and counted against the USD budget.
