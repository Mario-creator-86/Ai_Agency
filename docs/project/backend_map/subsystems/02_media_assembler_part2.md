## In one paragraph

This half of `app.media.assembler` is the money-spending and money-protecting back end of the film: it plans and buys the **motion staircase** (the hero test shot, then the rest of the motion, plus a single-scene redo) on Runway-style per-request vendors or in GPU rounds on RunPod Serverless; it **remembers per scene** what failed, what was paid and what the Owner last decided (`scene_memory`), so that a failed scene is never bought again on its own; it writes every vendor task as a `SUBMITTED` take the moment the vendor accepts it and **collects** those tasks later by id with GETs (never re-buying); it runs **clip QC** on every bought shot (local measurements plus a vision-model verdict read strictly by code, including whether a mistake the scene's movement was rewritten to fix is gone) with one paid retry inside the Owner-approved cap, and before that retry is bought (or the scene is handed on) it asks the **Director's second look** at the refused clip, which may keep it and so buy nothing more; it gates the rest of the motion on the **hero test shot** (the Owner's look, or clip QC's acceptance when the Owner's own paid-media rule allows); it records the Owner's **APPROVED / REJECTED look** on a take; it **deletes intermediates** once a film's QC report exists; and it implements the **scene redo** (still, motion, card, sound): a plan with its own `plan_hash`, the Owner's yes (or the Policy Kernel's unasked ALLOWED), the purchase lease, the film ceiling and the step budget hold, retirement of the old takes in the same transaction that spends the yes, a give-back of those takes when the redo delivers nothing, booking of what was spent, and a rejoin of the film with only the redone shot rebuilt. No function in this part writes a prompt to a language model; the LLM reaches this code only through the vision QC calls (`picture_qc.judge_clip`, `picture_qc.judge_still`), the Director's second look at a refused clip or still (`second_look._look`, through `fix_check`), and the Voice Artist's sound plan (via `repair_audio`), plus, as data written elsewhere, the Director's rewrite events and the takes her looks approved; every decision that spends money here is taken by deterministic code under the Owner's policy. The second look never buys anything itself: it withholds the retry or re-roll it was asked about, but a picture it keeps then counts as passed (a clip, record PASSED) or approved (a still), which later gates build on: a kept hero clip lets the Owner's own rule accept the hero and buy the rest of the motion (assembler.py:10006-10065), and a kept still may start paid motion (L9106-9132).

### 5. LLM decisions consumed as data: the Director's rewrite and the Director's looks

**The Director's rewrite.** `scene_memory` treats a `media.prompts_rewritten` event with `decided_by == "director"` as a decision about the scene (assembler.py:8786-8797): it lifts the scene's hold and restarts its paid-attempt count, so new purchases of that scene become possible again under the usual money gates. Three writers in `app.media.scene_rewrite` (outside this part) put `decided_by: "director"` on that event:

- the model rewrite ladder (the Director's brief `director_brief` scene_rewrite.py:1335, the Video Editor's rewrite `_editor_rewrite` scene_rewrite.py:1847, compiled into prompts, and the Director's review `review_rewrite` scene_rewrite.py:1650), stored only when her review answers TREATS (scene_rewrite.py:2274-2289, event at 2318): an **LLM** decision, checked by a second model call, not by code (code only refuses it when the film closed or the package changed meanwhile, scene_rewrite.py:2292-2303);
- the serial composer's deterministic re-wording (`_say_serial_words_again`, scene_rewrite.py:2707-2742) and the serial "technical refusal → same words, new seed" rung (scene_rewrite.py:2812-2832): **CODE_RULE** decisions recorded under the Director's name, no model involved.

Code in this part does not judge the rewrite's content: whatever the writer, the event is acted on as is to lift the hold, and only reopens the scene to the same `may_act`, cap, balance and attempt rules (the money gates are unchanged).

**The Director's look.** `director_qc.record_look` (called from runbook/stages.py:4433 after the model call `agents.reviews.review_bought_pictures`) writes a candidate the gate or clip QC had refused as `APPROVED` with `approved_by = "director"` and its failure code cleared (director_qc.py:2135-2147), after code checks the file is unchanged and the clip is eligible (`_choice_refused`). In this part such a take is simply an APPROVED take: `_bought_shots` starts paid motion from an APPROVED still (assembler.py:9106-9113) under the usual money gates, and `_motion_state` / `hero_gate` treat an APPROVED hero take as the Owner's look (assembler.py:9297-9298, 9999-10004; see Oddities). `_send_still_back_for_clip` protects only `approved_by == "owner"`, so clip QC can still reject a still the Director approved (assembler.py:9621).

**The Director's second look, as data.** A still her second look kept elsewhere (the stills step, or a still redo) is APPROVED with `approved_by = "director_look"` and no failure code (stills_stage.py:1017-1035): here it is an APPROVED still like any other, so `_bought_shots` may start paid motion from it (assembler.py:9106-9132) and `_send_still_back_for_clip` may still reject it (L9621). A clip her look kept inside `_check_shot` stays PAID (not APPROVED) with a clip-QC record PASSED and `accepted_by = "director_look"` (L10365-10373): `hero_gate` reads that record as clip QC's pass (L10006-10019) and `_film_after_motion` as a passing redone shot (L15786).

## Oddities and gaps

1. **Raw price table fallback.** `_measured_still_spend` passes `table or load_price_table()` to `record_measured_prices` (assembler.py:16714), while every plan in this part prices through `shooting_sheet.price_table_for` (L9209, L11533, L13814). It is used only for recording measured prices, not to price a purchase.
2. **"runway" hard-coded in measured-price recording and booking defaults.** `record_measured_prices` receives `{"kind": "runway", ...}` for both the motion step (L12430-12431) and the still redo (L16704-16705) whatever the vendor; `_book_redo` and `_redo_interrupted` default `billing_provider` to `"runway"` (L15508, L15536) unless `_paid_redo` set `"runpod"` from `billing_name(...)` (L15300-15301).
3. **RunPod shot request resolution is fixed.** `_ShotRounds._request` always sends 1080×1920 at 24 fps (L11070-11073), while the plan prices at `resolution` = `settings.motion_resolution`, which may be `"720p"` (config.py:279; assembler.py:11522-11524).
4. **Two motion purchase loops with different per-shot checks.** The sequential loop checks `_bought_elsewhere` on the first attempt (L12055-12066) and reads the balance and the uncounted drop before every shot (L12028-12054); the RunPod `_ShotRounds` path does neither per shot (it has the step-start balance read L11636 and `run_rounds`' `cap_left`). The hold flag differs too: the sequential path treats LOW_BALANCE as free (L12332-12335), the rounds path only free failure kinds with no spend (L11383-11386), and only the rounds path adds `runpod_items` (L11401). The rounds path also has no vendor failure ladder: no SAFETY send-back of the still, no `_suggest_move`, no STUDIO_DEFECT branch; any worker error is retried once on the same seed, then the scene goes NEEDS_OWNER as `BAD_OUTPUT` (L11305-11316).
5. **Two still-redo purchase loops.** `_StillRedoBuyer` re-implements "the stills step's rules for one scene" (docstring L16341-16343) for per-request vendors, while `_redo_still_rounds` reuses `stills_stage._StillRounds` for RunPod (L16264). Since 5fc7468 a round is judged and settled by one shared function (`stills._settle_round`, L16571-16579) and the plan by one builder (`stills._still_plan`, L16157), but the candidate-buying loop still lives in two places (this file and stills_stage), and the class docstring (L16341-16343: «at most two re-rolls …, then the Owner») does not mention the Director's look or the rewrite outcome.
6. **No attempt limit on redos, including unasked ones.** `over_attempts` is always False for `scene_redo` (L11766) and `_redo_still_rounds` passes `limit=10**9` with the comment «His press is his decision» (L16278-16280); the same paths run unasked from the runbook's Director repair (`unasked=True, director_repair=True`, runbook/stages.py:5036-5060). Redos are bounded by their cap and the runbook's per-film redo caps (ADR-AUTOMATIC-REDOS, 16_DECISION_LOG.md:4112).
7. **Deferred clip-QC retry recorded before it is bought.** In the rounds path the first check's `defer` buy returns `(None, None)` (L11215-11218), so `qc_clip_with_retry` returns ESCALATED with `attempts=2` and `retry_credits=price` (qc.py:2699-2711) for a retry that has not been bought yet; when the retry is never bought (stop, cap), that record becomes the scene's `clip_qc` (L11368-11374). That first `_check_shot` call also runs `fix_check.clip_settled` on the first clip as not kept (L10389-10401), so a motion-layer item that persists withdraws the rewrite's lessons before the deferred retry is bought; in the sequential path `clip_settled` runs once, on the retry's report.
8. **A redone shot is recorded as `motion`.** `MOTION_STEP_TAKE_KINDS.get(step, "motion")` (L11600) gives `scene_redo` takes kind `motion` even when the scene's previous shot was the hero `test_shot`.
9. **`decide_take`'s "Only he decides" is a string comparison.** It raises `PermissionError` unless `decided_by == "owner"` (L9730); who the caller is, is enforced by the router dependency `require_owner` (router.py:319), not here.
10. **`check_unchecked_shots` silently skips without FFmpeg.** It returns `[]` (L10842-10844), so paid shots stay unchecked; per its own docstring such shots never enter a PRODUCTION film (L10836-10838).
11. **A SUBMITTED take with no task id waits 24 h.** `collect_submitted_takes` records `problem = "no task id"` and leaves it SUBMITTED until `max_age_seconds`, then LOST with a letter (L10717-10718, L10770-10802).
12. **Docstring vs callers.** The comment at L8481-8483 says studio-defect holds are released «by a startup or watchdog pass»; the only caller in `backend/app` is the watchdog (watchdog.py:916).
13. **Still paid-attempt count is approximate without task ids.** `_paid_attempts` counts loose still candidates as `ceil(n / 2)` requests (L8648-8650).
14. **Identity rewrite on give-back.** `give_back_retired_takes` may overwrite an Owner-approved take's `prompt_hash` with the restored scene's identity (`bound_to_picture`, L13578-13586, L13652-13653), keeping the old hash only on the event (`previous_prompt_hash`, L13672).
15. **Permanent deletions.** `release_intermediates` (L12658) and `sweep_failed_intermediates` (L12803) `unlink` files; the first is gated by the QC report, sha256 match and re-derivability, the second by an opt-in setting (default off) and a 7-day age.
16. **Size.** `run_motion_step` is 1,062 lines (L11426-12487) with closures (`buy_retry`, two `submitted`) that bind loop variables through default arguments; `_redo_still` is 339 lines (L15876-16214) and `give_back_retired_takes` 290.
17. **`hero_gate` default credit rate.** When the plan carries no `credit_usd`, 0.01 USD per credit is assumed for the cap shown to the kernel (L9967).
18. **Static analysis limits.** inventory.json marks `qc.check_clip`, `qc.qc_clip_with_retry` and `stills_stage._judge_candidate` as not reaching an LLM because the vision judge and the Director's look are passed as callables; the real LLM paths run through them (see LLM involvement).
19. **`REDO.OWNER` is written by unasked redos too, and lifts holds.** Both redo paths retire with `REDO_FAILURE_CODE = "REDO.OWNER"` / `REDO.STILL_REPLACED` whoever started the redo (L11703-11714, L16075-16097), including the runbook's unasked Director repair (runbook/stages.py:5036-5060). `scene_memory` reads every take with those codes as a decision about the scene (L8751-8755): the hold lifts and the paid-attempt count restarts, although the comment at L8455-8457 lists «a redo he approved (takes retired REDO.*)» among «his own acts». The retirement event keeps `owner_press` and `director_repair` (L13204-13205), but `scene_memory` does not read them.
20. **The hero gate reads any APPROVED take as the Owner's look.** `_motion_state` sets `accepted_by = "owner"` for any APPROVED shot (L9297-9298) and `hero_gate` returns APPROVED «aprobat de Proprietar» for any APPROVED hero take (L9999-10004), without reading `approved_by`. A test shot approved by the Director's look (`director_qc.record_look`, `approved_by = "director"`, director_qc.py:2145-2147; her look offers shots too, director_qc.py:1923, 2000) therefore opens the rest of the motion without the Owner's look or the clip-QC/rule acceptance; the motion purchase itself still goes through `may_act` and its own cap.
21. **"The Director's decision" is sometimes code.** The `media.prompts_rewritten` events with `decided_by: "director"` that lift a hold (L8786-8797) are written both by the model rewrite ladder and by deterministic serial rungs (scene_rewrite.py:2707-2742, 2812-2832) that involve no model.
22. **A vendor SAFETY refusal rejects even an Owner-approved still.** `_send_still_back` sets the still REJECTED `MOTION.<code>` with no `approved_by` check (L9532-9569), while `_send_still_back_for_clip` spares a still the Owner approved (L9621-9625).
23. **A clip the Director's look kept is recorded as a clip-QC pass.** `_check_shot` rewrites the last report's verdict to PASSED with `accepted_by = "director_look"` (L10365-10373), keeping clip QC's blocks on it. Readers that test `verdict == "PASSED"` cannot tell it from a measured pass: `hero_gate` (L10006-10019) then lets the Owner's rule accept a hero clip QC refused, and `_accept_hero` records that as `media.hero_accepted_by_clip_qc`; `_film_after_motion` (L15786) rejoins a redone shot QC refused. The take stays PAID, while a still her look keeps is set APPROVED `approved_by = "director_look"` (stills_stage.py:1017-1035).
24. **«The gate approved one» may be the Director's look.** `_judge_paid_stills_first` ends the press with a reason saying the waiting pictures «au fost judecate acum, fără plată, și una a fost aprobată» (L15682-15686) whenever a new APPROVED still appears; since `settle_stills` → `_settle_pending` also asks her look on a round the gate refused whole (stills_stage.py:1254-1260), that approval may be hers, which the reason does not say.
25. **Her confirmations are not always returned.** `_redo_still` merges `{**outcome, "director_confirmed": buyer.confirmed}` (L16197-16199): `buyer.confirmed` holds only the confirmations given before a re-roll (L16586-16587), and when it is non-empty it replaces the final round's own `director_confirmed` from `_settle_round` (stills_stage.py:1182-1183). The RunPod path returns neither: `_redo_still_rounds` keeps only `status`, `reason`, `reasons`, `take_id`, `score`, `failure_kind`, `failure_code` of the scene's entry (L16321-16323), so `director_confirmed` (stills_stage.py:1693) and her `approved_by` are dropped from the redo's result.
26. **A still redo's `rewrite` outcome is written as an Owner hold.** When `still_gate.round_step` says `rewrite` (still_gate.py:1160-1183), `_settle_round` returns NEEDS_OWNER with `rewrite: true` (stills_stage.py:1173-1181); `_StillRedoBuyer.run` writes it as a holding `media.still_needs_owner` without the `rewrite` flag (L16590-16608), and no code in `backend/app` reads that flag: the hold lifts on the next decision `scene_memory` reads, in practice the Director's rewrite event (L8786-8797), or else the Owner's take decision or model choice (L8749-8785).
27. **The second look is asked on the clip's refusal even when no retry could be bought.** `qc_clip_with_retry` asks her before the ESCALATE branch (qc.py:2670-2697), so with `check_unchecked_shots`' `cap=0` (L10888) or past the step's cap a refused clip can still become PAID by her look; nothing is bought either way.
