## Root causes

### Prompts that ask for forbidden content (positive and negative)

Our own code put the people into the prompts. When a bible forbids faces, the compiler does not remove people. It adds a sentence asking for them: "Any people appear only as small distant figures seen from behind, faces turned away." (backend/app/media/prompt_agent.py:2303-2305, inserted at :2964). It adds this whenever a loose word list finds a "person" in the plan. In scene 1 the only "person" was the word "finger" in "a finger's-width gap" (prompt_agent.py:2600, the `fingers?` entry), and that phrase came from the Director's own brief. The same compiler adds "Faces stay turned away from the camera." to every non-veo motion prompt (prompt_agent.py:2313, :2826). The still re-roll adds "figures small and distant, seen from behind, faces not visible" (still_gate.py:250 via stills_stage.py:786). The linter demands such a sentence and even suggests those exact words (prompt_lint.py:895-909). All three LLM instructions tell the writers to show people from behind or their hands (prompt_agent.py:393-394, scene_rewrite.py:512-514 and :666-668).

At 10:36:46 the Director's brief explicitly said to delete the people sentence. Kael's rewrite at 10:36:55 removed it. The compiler put it back (event media.prompts_rewritten at 10:37:03.537), and the GPU session opened at 10:37:04 with no check in between. The rewrite also added "Faces stay turned away" to the motion prompt, which had not carried it before. All 6 scene-1 stills ever made show people seen from behind. The approved one, 4be4364e, shows 4 people with 2 profiles partly visible. All 7 clips started from it, so they all carried faces.

The negative channels give no protection:
- The video model never gets a negative prompt. All 64 LTX requests in the database have none, and the LTX graph runs at cfg 1.0, where a negative has no effect anyway.
- The still negative (only since 2026-10-12 09:33) has "visible faces, portrait" but no person/people/figure words. The plan's own "people, figures, faces or hands anywhere in frame" item was cut off by the 400-character limit.

I reproduced all of this offline: compile_prompts on the stored plan gives back the same still and motion prompts. The fix makes faceless mean no human word in any positive prompt, still or motion. The compiler removes people instead of hiding them. Both negatives carry a fixed list of human words. A deterministic gate before every still or clip purchase refuses a prompt that still names a person and logs the word. The LLM instructions and the Director's brief are rewritten to match, and the Director sees the exact prompt and negative that were sent.

- The compiler treats 'faces forbidden' as 'people seen from behind'. It writes people in: PEOPLE_SENTENCE, FACES_CONSTRAINT, hide_faces «seen from behind», _cast_sentence «a figure seen from behind». It decides whether a scene has people with a loose word list, so a measure idiom ('finger's width') counts as a person (prompt_agent.py:2303-2313, 2569-2615, 2826, 2952-2964, 2994-3005).
- Every layer meant to enforce the rule repeats it. The linter BLOCKs a still that does not state the face rule and suggests 'figures small and distant, seen from behind' (prompt_lint.py:884-909). The still re-roll asks for figures (still_gate.py:250). The shot writer, the Director's brief and the rewriter are all told to show people from behind or their hands (prompt_agent.py:393-394; scene_rewrite.py:512-514, 666-668). The LLMs are told these are fixed sentences they cannot change (scene_rewrite.py:1102-1152), and the 2026-10-12 decision rejected removing them (16_DECISION_LOG.md:39-41).
- Nothing deterministic sits between the compiled words and the purchase. The 10:37 rewrite was compiled and bought within one second, with no check that the brief's 'delete «…»' was honoured and no check for human words. lint_motion_prompt has no bible parameter, so motion prompts are never checked.
- The negative channels are unused or ineffective. The video model never gets a negative (render_motion returns one only for veo), and the LTX graph runs at cfg 1.0, where a negative has no effect anyway. The still negative has no person words, and its 400-character limit is filled by irrelevant generic items, so the plan's 'people…' item is cut.
- The bible's face rule is found by regex in free text through several divergent detectors (stills_stage._bible_bans_faces and lint.bible_face_rule). For this film it was found only by accident, and 'No people appear in any shot' is not recognised at all.
- Quality control treats back-turned people as compliant (picture_qc.py:2020-2021), and YuNet misses partial profiles. A still with 4 people (4be4364e) was approved and animated 7 times, and every clip inherited the faces from its first frame.
