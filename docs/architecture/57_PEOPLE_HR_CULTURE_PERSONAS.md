# People Operations, HR, Culture & Personas

## Purpose

Manage a growing AI workforce while preserving operational clarity, narrative character continuity and company culture.

HR/People Ops is optional at small scale and may be created by the CEO when workforce complexity justifies it.

## Possible roles

- Head of People / HR Director
- Recruiter
- Workforce Planner
- Onboarding Agent
- Performance/Development Agent
- Culture & Events Coordinator

HR may recommend actions but does not bypass CEO/governance authority.

## When HR becomes useful

Possible triggers:
- workforce exceeds configured threshold;
- CEO has too many direct reports;
- frequent hiring/firing;
- many probationary agents;
- department restructuring;
- recurring performance disputes;
- increased narrative/persona complexity.

## Operational identity vs narrative persona

Keep these separate.

### OperationalIdentity
Determines:
- role;
- permissions;
- tools;
- provider/model;
- capabilities;
- tasks;
- performance;
- employment status.

### NarrativePersona
Used for:
- office presentation;
- serial dialogue style;
- personality;
- social interactions;
- recurring jokes;
- birthday;
- preferences;
- relationships.

NarrativePersona must never expand OperationalIdentity permissions or lower factual/safety standards.

## Persona fields

Potential fields:
- public/display name;
- fictional/in-universe birthday;
- optional fictional age policy;
- personality traits;
- humor style;
- communication style;
- conflict style;
- likes;
- dislikes;
- favorite office object;
- hobby/interests;
- work quirks;
- strengths;
- weaknesses;
- catchphrases;
- preferred social style;
- voice profile;
- visual identity reference;
- relationship tendencies.

Birthdays are in-universe narrative metadata, not claims about a real model/company.

## Persona stability

Traits should be versioned and relatively stable.

Do not randomly rewrite personalities each episode.

Major persona evolution should have:
- story reason;
- version;
- date;
- continuity note.

## Relationships

Maintain a narrative relationship graph:
- rapport;
- trust;
- rivalry;
- mentorship;
- tension;
- friendship;
- recent interactions.

This graph is narrative/cultural state, not a factual evaluation of real AI providers.

Relationship changes may be influenced by committed:
- operational disagreements;
- collaborative successes;
- company events;
- serialized world events.

## Onboarding

New employee:
- operational configuration;
- probation status;
- character identity;
- persona;
- desk/department;
- onboarding event;
- role charter acknowledgement;
- test tasks.

Onboarding can become serial content.

## Offboarding

Termination:
- access/tool revocation;
- task reassignment;
- archive;
- character leaves office;
- continuity updated;
- optional farewell story event if appropriate.

## Culture system

Possible values:
- truth before virality;
- curiosity;
- experimentation;
- accountability;
- cost awareness;
- collaboration.

Culture values may influence narrative tone and soft decision heuristics but never override hard gates.

## Team events

HR/Culture may propose:
- team building;
- birthday;
- milestone celebration;
- awards;
- offsite;
- hack day;
- no-production day;
- onboarding day.

Actual scheduling uses Company Calendar/Event Engine and CEO policy.
