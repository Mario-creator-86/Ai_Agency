# Financial Dashboard & Forecasting

## Owner / CEO dashboard

Show at minimum:

### Treasury
- Available VR$
- Restricted VR$
- Receivables
- Pending payouts
- Debt
- Next debt service
- Runway days/months

### Current period
- Settled revenue
- Actual operating costs
- Virtual procurement spend
- Provider costs
- Marketing costs
- Net VR$ cash movement

### Revenue mix
- Channel A
- Channel B
- TikTok
- Instagram/Facebook
- Telegram
- audience payments
- sponsorships
- other.

### Unit economics
- revenue per published video;
- cost per video;
- cost per 1K views;
- gross contribution per content item;
- rework/failure cost;
- marketing efficiency.

### Procurement
- open requests;
- quoted spend;
- approved commitments;
- assets purchased;
- new vs used savings.

### Debt
- principal outstanding;
- accrued interest;
- payments due;
- capacity remaining;
- stress status.

## Forecasting

Forecast horizons:
- 7 days tactical;
- 30 days;
- 90 days;
- 180 days.

Scenarios:
- BEAR;
- BASE;
- BULL;
- optional custom scenario.

Inputs:
- historical settled revenue;
- channel growth trend;
- upload schedule;
- monetization RPM/rates when evidenced;
- seasonality if supported;
- provider cost trend;
- marketing plans;
- staffing;
- procurement commitments;
- debt service.

Output includes:
- point estimate;
- range;
- confidence;
- assumptions;
- last-updated;
- forecast-vs-actual error.

## Forecast governance

Forecasts are advisory.
They do not mint VR$.
CEO may plan against them only within explicit policy/reserve rules.

## Public display

Owner can choose:
- exact;
- rounded;
- indexed/gamified;
- hidden.
