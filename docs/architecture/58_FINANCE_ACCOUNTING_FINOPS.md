# Finance, Accounting, Treasury & FinOps Department — V5

## Mission

Operate the studio as a financially constrained organization, not an infinite-budget simulation.

Finance manages the **VR$ economy**, which is anchored one-to-one to verified USD economic value while remaining separate from real-world payment authority.

## Core rule

`1 verified real USD inflow = 1 VR$ credit.`

`1 verified real USD actual operating cost = 1 VR$ cost.`

`1 USD real market price selected for an in-world purchase = 1 VR$ purchase cost.`

VR$ is a virtual management/world currency. It is not cryptocurrency, legal tender, stored-value money, or a bank balance.

## Financial truth model

### 1. External Evidence Layer
Read-only or Owner-attested real-world economic facts:
- platform settlement;
- donation/payment settlement;
- sponsor payment;
- Owner funding;
- provider invoice/usage cost;
- refund/chargeback.

### 2. VR Ledger
Double-entry virtual company books.

### 3. Forecast Layer
Estimates and scenarios that never automatically create spendable VR$.

## Status distinctions

Revenue can be:
- ESTIMATED;
- ACCRUED/RECEIVABLE;
- PENDING_PAYOUT;
- SETTLED;
- REVERSED.

Only eligible SETTLED or Owner-confirmed funding enters spendable treasury.

## Revenue recognition / minting

Every eligible source uses a unique evidence key.

Example:
YouTube settlement: `$734.22`
→ `RevenueEvidence(settled)`
→ eligible mint transaction
→ `+734.22 VR$`

If the platform later reverses `$25`:
→ reversal evidence
→ `-25 VR$`
→ treasury/risk consequences.

Never mint from views alone or from forecast revenue.

## Owner funding

Owner may record:

### CAPITAL_CONTRIBUTION
- increases VR$ cash/equity;
- no interest.

### OWNER_LOAN
- increases VR$ cash and liability;
- stores rate, maturity, payment schedule;
- accrues VR$ interest.

The application records the event but does not move the actual money.

## Real provider costs

Connected paid LLM/video/TTS/cloud usage may incur actual external bills.

Track:
- estimated usage cost immediately when available;
- actual billed/reconciled amount later;
- variance;
- content/agent attribution.

Hard provider budgets remain enforceable.

## Procurement approval

Finance checks:
- available VR$;
- reserve requirements;
- existing commitments;
- runway;
- department budget;
- debt service;
- purchase necessity;
- new vs used alternatives;
- price evidence quality.

Finance can reject a purchase even if nominal VR$ is technically available when runway policy would be violated.

## Treasury stress

Thresholds may trigger:
- advisory warning;
- discretionary spending freeze;
- hiring freeze;
- provider downgrade proposal;
- team-building/upgrade postponement;
- financing proposal;
- restructuring plan.

## Forecasting

Generate BASE/BULL/BEAR forecasts for 30/90/180 days using:
- channel trends;
- platform payout history;
- production schedule;
- provider cost model;
- marketing plans;
- financing obligations;
- procurement commitments.

Store assumptions and confidence. Forecasts are not cash.

## Privacy

Exact VR$ can reveal real income because of the 1:1 peg.

Owner controls public disclosure:
- PRIVATE_EXACT;
- PUBLIC_ROUNDED;
- PUBLIC_INDEXED;
- HIDDEN.

## Serial potential

Finance is a story engine:
- CEO vs CFO on a chair;
- budget freeze;
- surprisingly profitable episode;
- audience donation funds an office upgrade;
- debt negotiation;
- Procurement finds a bargain.

Story must remain grounded in posted financial/world events.
