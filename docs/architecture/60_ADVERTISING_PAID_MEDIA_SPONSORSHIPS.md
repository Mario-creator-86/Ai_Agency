# Advertising, Paid Media & Sponsorships

## Scope

This is a later-stage growth function.

It may begin inside Marketing and split into its own department when spend/revenue justifies it.

## Potential roles

- Paid Media Manager
- Campaign Buyer
- Sponsorship/Partnership Manager
- Brand Integration Reviewer
- Ad Creative Agent

## Paid media

Capabilities may include:
- campaign planning;
- creative variants;
- budget proposals;
- platform targeting;
- performance analysis;
- pause/scale recommendations.

Actual ad buying requires real supported APIs/accounts/permissions.

Do not automate paid spend without explicit budget policies and risk limits.

## Sponsorships

Future workflow:
opportunity
→ brand fit
→ commercial terms (Owner involvement)
→ legal/disclosure review
→ content integration plan
→ production
→ sponsor approval if required
→ publish
→ performance report.

The AI CEO must not sign binding contracts or accept legal terms on behalf of the Owner.

## Disclosure

Sponsored/paid content must use required platform/legal disclosures.

No hidden advertising.

## Separation from organic marketing

Keep:
- organic campaign;
- paid media;
- sponsor campaign
as distinct campaign types with distinct attribution and budgets.


# V5 Paid media money boundary

Because the studio currently may not autonomously spend real money, actual paid-ad buying is disabled by default.

Marketing may model a proposed paid campaign in VR$ and seek Owner approval, but no external spend occurs unless a future explicit product-scope change adds controlled real payment authority.

Sponsorship income, when actually settled through an external business/payment account, may be ingested as RevenueEvidence and converted 1:1 to VR$.
