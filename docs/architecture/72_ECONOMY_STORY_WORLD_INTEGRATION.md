The Owner explicitly supersedes the earlier top-down/3D headquarters and active Treasury,
virtual financing and VendorShop product requirements. The active interface is an original
browser-rendered, side-view pixel studio; no Blender, Unreal or GPU stream is required.

provenance, QA, encrypted credentials, Owner policy/approvals/STOP, durable workers,
scheduling and recovery. Public Treasury/funding/minting/FX/virtual-ledger commands and
screens are retired. Historical data and internal accounting compatibility remain preserved.
Existing permissions are not raised automatically by developer AUTORUN.

This scope takes precedence over conflicting visual/economy requirements below and in the
older architecture package. Record: ../project/16_DECISION_LOG.md, ADR-SIMPLE-PIXEL-STUDIO.

## Why economy matters narratively

The economy turns channel success/failure into visible consequences.

Examples:
- viral Short → settled income later → larger VR$ treasury;
- CFO approves new meeting room;
- Procurement finds second-hand furniture;
- CEO over-spends → freeze;
- fan-funded idea pays for new equipment;
- Owner loan saves the studio;
- low cash cancels team building;
- debt repayment causes tension.

## Canon chain

Real economic evidence / committed world action
→ ledger event
→ company historical event
→ Daily Studio Log
→ Showrunner candidate
→ marketing teaser if public-safe.

## No narrative money creation

Showrunner cannot create a financial fact merely because it is entertaining.

"We are broke" is only valid if treasury/stress state supports it.

"We made $10,000" is only valid if disclosure mode and settled evidence support it.

## Virtual purchase wording

A serial may say:
"The studio bought a new chair from VendorShop for 420 VR$."

It must not imply:
"We ordered a real chair from Amazon and it arrived at our physical office"
unless that happened outside the app and Owner explicitly records it.

## Finance characters

CFO, Procurement and VendorShop can become recurring characters whose genuine operational decisions generate comedy/conflict.
