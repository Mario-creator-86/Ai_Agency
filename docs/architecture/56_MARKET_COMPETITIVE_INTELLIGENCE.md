# Market & Competitive Intelligence Department

## Purpose

Continuously observe what audiences, creators, formats and markets appear to be responding to, then turn evidence into experiments rather than imitation.

Possible department names:
- Market Intelligence
- Audience Intelligence
- Strategy Lab
- Competitive Intelligence

The CEO may create this department when justified.

## Core roles

Possible:
- Head of Market Intelligence
- Trend Analyst
- Competitor Scout
- Geographic Audience Analyst
- Format Analyst
- Platform Trend Analyst

## Inputs

Public and authorized data such as:
- our platform analytics;
- public competitor posts/videos;
- public trend tools;
- platform search/discovery;
- public news/trend sources;
- geographic audience data when available;
- comments/community feedback.

Do not bypass:
- logins;
- anti-bot protections;
- paywalls;
- platform restrictions.

## Outputs

Create structured objects:

### MarketSignal
- signal;
- evidence;
- platform;
- geography;
- freshness;
- confidence;
- estimated relevance;
- expiry.

### CompetitorObservation
- competitor/entity;
- public source;
- observed format;
- hook;
- topic;
- cadence;
- engagement indicators;
- uncertainty;
- rights/copying warning.

### OpportunityHypothesis
Example:
"German viewers in our current audience appear to complete short science explainers at a higher rate."

Must include:
- evidence;
- sample;
- alternative explanations;
- confidence;
- proposed experiment;
- estimated cost/risk.

## Anti-copy rule

Competitive Intelligence may learn patterns, not plagiarize.

Never:
- copy scripts;
- clone protected characters;
- re-upload media;
- reproduce distinctive creative expression;
- assume a competitor's style is legally reusable.

Prefer abstraction:
- "faster hook"
- "question framing"
- "8–12 second teaser"
rather than imitation.

## CEO decision path

Market Intelligence does not directly change the company strategy unless delegated.

Typical path:
Signal
→ analysis
→ recommendation
→ CEO review
→ experiment
→ measured outcome
→ strategy update if justified.

## Serial integration

A strategic discovery can become a real office storyline.

Example:
Market Intelligence reports:
"German viewers show stronger retention on X format."

Office Serial:
"The Strategy team brought the CEO a surprising Germany report. Now the entire studio is waiting to see whether the CEO approves the experiment."

The episode must accurately reflect decision state:
- recommendation pending;
- approved;
- rejected;
- experiment running.

Do not depict a decision as final when it is still pending.
