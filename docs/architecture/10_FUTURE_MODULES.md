# Future Modules — Architecture Hooks Only

Do not let future features distract from building a real autonomous studio first.

## Public fan portal

Potential:
- safe live office;
- episode archive;
- topic voting;
- decor voting;
- selected Director decision voting.

Use a sanitized backend projection. Never expose secrets/private logs.

## Paid idea pitches / insights

Future flow:
- user pays through an Owner-enabled compliant payment provider;
- payment settles externally;
- RevenueEvidence is created;
- settled amount may mint VR$ 1:1;
- submission is moderated;
- Director receives pitch;
- normal research/quality policy still applies;
- payment never guarantees production unless Owner-defined terms explicitly say so.

## Economy status

The VR$ Economy, Treasury, Finance approval, Procurement, VendorShop, market-price sourcing and virtual financing are **planned core architecture**, not merely future placeholders.

See documents 66–72.

Future economy extensions may include:
- fan-funded room campaigns;
- richer VendorShop catalog;
- virtual resale/auction;
- depreciation/repair systems;
- multi-studio consolidation;
- advanced accounting exports.

## Fan votes

Potential:
- topic;
- decor;
- non-critical experiments;
- selected strategy input.

Owner/Director policy remains final for safety/quality.

## Merchandise/IP

Future opportunity:
- character merchandise;
- branded office items;
- show-related products.

Not required for core application.

## Community moderation

Before public participation:
- account/session model;
- rate limiting;
- spam prevention;
- abuse/report flow;
- moderation queue;
- privacy/consent;
- public display-name rules.

## Payment isolation and no-real-spend rule

Inbound audience payments may be accepted only through Owner-enabled external processors and then mirrored through RevenueEvidence.

The autonomous studio does not:
- store raw card data;
- spend real money on VendorShop items;
- place external product orders;
- apply for real financing.

## World Builder

VendorShop items should be renderable through supported asset/world schemas.

A store item must define:
- selected market quote;
- asset specification;
- footprint;
- allowed room types;
- placement rules;
- VR$ price;
- story metadata.

## Fan influence limits

Fans may influence content/decoration, but cannot vote away:
- rights checks;
- factual gates;
- security;
- Owner policy;
- platform compliance;
- financial hard gates.

## Recognized future operational departments/modules

Stable extension points exist for:
- Marketing & Communications;
- Market / Competitive Intelligence;
- HR / People Ops;
- Finance / FinOps;
- Legal / Compliance;
- Advertising / Paid Media;
- Company Calendar / Events;
- Narrative Persona / Relationships.

They do not all need implementation in the MVP.
