# V5 Revision Report — Economy, Treasury & VendorShop

## Why V5 exists

The studio previously separated actual external money, estimates and an internal fictional economy. V5 makes the economy a first-class, realistic subsystem tied one-to-one to real USD evidence and real-world market prices, while preserving a strict prohibition on autonomous real-world spending.

## Core additions

1. `1 USD = 1 VR$` eligibility/mint rule.
2. External Evidence vs VR Ledger vs Forecast separation.
3. Double-entry immutable virtual ledger.
4. Revenue settlement, reversals and chargeback handling.
5. Multi-platform monetization adapters.
6. Owner capital contribution and Owner loans.
7. Virtual market-referenced financing with interest and debt capacity.
8. Finance/Treasury dashboard with runway and scenarios.
9. Procurement workflow using current market prices.
10. VendorShop for virtual purchases at selected real-world reference prices.
11. NEW vs USED product sourcing.
12. Asset Architect to turn approved purchases into consistent office assets.
13. Financial stress, budget freezes and insolvency-style consequences.
14. Story/marketing integration with committed economy events.
15. Financial privacy controls.

## Important safety/realism clarification

VendorShop never orders the real product. Financing never applies for a real loan. VR$ is not a bank balance. The system can read/record real economic evidence and can incur already-authorized provider usage costs, but it has no autonomous outbound real-money payment authority.
