# V4 Revision Report — Organizational Expansion

## Why this revision exists

The studio is no longer only a set of content-production agents. The desired product is an evolving AI company that can grow new teams, communicate across multiple social channels, develop culture and create story-worthy company events.

## Key changes

### 1. Dynamic departments
The CEO can create, merge and dissolve departments using real backend organization state. Department creation requires charter, manager, budget, permissions, KPI and review.

### 2. Marketing & Communications
Added first-class Marketing architecture for:
- YouTube
- TikTok
- Instagram
- Facebook
- Telegram

Marketing can produce truthful same-day teasers for tomorrow's Office Serial.

### 3. Truthful clickbait
Curiosity is optimized, but facts/stakes cannot be invented. Teasers must link to committed events and pass safety/continuity/policy gates.

### 4. Market Intelligence
Added a team that can study audience/competitor/trend signals and send hypotheses to the CEO. It learns patterns without copying protected creative content.

### 5. HR / People Ops
Added optional HR for larger workforce, onboarding, probation, staffing, culture and company events.

### 6. Human-like AI personas
Added a separate NarrativePersona layer with:
- birthday
- traits
- likes/dislikes
- quirks
- relationships
- voice/visual continuity.

Operational permissions remain separate.

### 7. Finance / FinOps
Added clear separation between:
- real external money;
- estimated revenue;
- fictional studio economy.

### 8. Legal / Compliance
Added content/marketing/IP/sponsor/rights review and compliance holds.

### 9. Advertising / Sponsorship
Defined later-stage paid media and sponsorship workflow without prematurely enabling autonomous spend or contract signing.

### 10. Team building and company events
Added Company Calendar/Event Engine.
A milestone such as a 1M-view Short can trigger a team-building proposal.
An approved event may pause ordinary production for the day while essential reliability/security services remain active.
The Office Serial can then cover the event.

### 11. Three truth classes
To preserve realism:
- OPERATIONAL_EVENT
- COMPANY_WORLD_EVENT
- STORY_EMBELLISHMENT

This allows a fictional office universe to have birthdays/team building without confusing those with real-world physical events.

### 12. Anti-bloat
The CEO must not create teams merely for entertainment. Organizational growth is evaluated against workload, value, cost and coordination overhead.

## Recommendation

Include all of this **now in architecture and data contracts**, but implement departments progressively after the core studio and content pipeline are reliable.
