Use case: stylized-concept. One production transparent PIXEL SPRITE ATLAS.
Art direction: original lovingly crafted narrative adventure pixel sprites, natural anatomy and nuanced pixel-cluster shading inspired by the quality of A Space for the Unbound. Warm twilight palette matching a richly illustrated timber office and old city. Fine selective dark contours, believable metal/frame construction, delicate hair and cloth folds, anatomically coherent adult rider and expressive animals. Absolutely not icons, block rectangles, chibi, low-detail polygon figures, caricature noses, 3D, smooth vector or painted blurry art.
Exact layout: square atlas, 4 equal columns by 4 equal rows, 16 independent centered tiles; prefer 2048x2048 total with 512x512 cells. Genuinely transparent background including spokes/triangle spaces; no text, no labels, no border/grid lines, no environment, no baked checkerboard. Safe empty margins. Everything facing RIGHT. In each row, same object/identity/size across all four cells, ground baseline 88% cell height.
ROW 1 — same bicycle and same adult rider in FOUR sequential pedalling keyframes, crank angles 0,90,180,270 degrees. Entire bicycle visible with exactly two round spoked wheels, slender correctly connected diamond frame, fork, saddle, handlebar, crank and chain, basket woven with small gaps, proper front/rear mudguards. Copper-bronze vintage bicycle. Slim adult man in muted teal jacket and charcoal trousers, warm brown shoes, textured dark hair, actual small face and hands, head at normal human proportion, inclined relaxed torso, arms bend naturally toward handlebars. Both feet ALWAYS attached to opposite pedals, knees bend coherently. The bike should be ~390px wide inside each 512px cell. Rotating spokes differ across frames, wheel diameters consistent. No duplicated or broken spokes/limbs.
ROW 2 — same elegant small cream-and-ginger cat in four coherent WALK right keyframes with anatomically correct paws, legs, ears, expressive eyes, whiskers, nose, chest fur clusters and curved tail. No oversized head, no disconnected head. Each cat ~240px wide in tile, naturally feline with graceful body.
ROW 3 — same cream-and-ginger cat, four poses in order: sitting upright tail around paws; grooming a raised forepaw; full-body stretch with lowered forelegs and raised hips; sitting quietly with half-closed/blinking eyes. Real neck connects head and body. Consistent face, markings and scale with row2. Each cat same overall size in tile.
ROW 4 — same small warm brown-and-cream dog in four WALK right keyframes. A realistic small mixed-breed dog with soft hanging ears, gentle expressive face, natural muzzle, moving tail, distinct paws and collar. ~270px wide in tile, same scale across row. Coherent gait with supporting paws on ground and lifted paws clearing ground. Neither toy nor icon.
Rich fine sprite detail that remains readable when downscaled to a game scene, precise silhouettes, true transparent alpha. Consistent soft warm upper-left light, small contact shadows only.
